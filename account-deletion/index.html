JajanTracker Account Deletion

 

You may request the permanent deletion of your JajanTracker account and all associated application data at any time.

1. Request Account Deletion

To request account deletion, please send an email to:
📧 jeremy.adrian@hendrickprasdito.com

Use the email address registered with your JajanTracker account and include the following subject:
JajanTracker – Account Deletion Request

Once your request has been verified, your account will be scheduled for permanent deletion within approximately 48 hours. You will receive an email notification when the deletion process has been initiated.

Additional verification may be required for security purposes.

2. Data Deletion and Recovery

Once your account has been deleted, all associated application data, including budgets, expenses, expense histories, and other related records, will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable laws or regulations, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

3. Cancellation of Account Deletion

You may cancel the scheduled deletion by signing in to the JajanTracker application after receiving the deletion notification email and before the deletion process is completed.

Signing in during this period will automatically cancel the deletion process, allowing you to continue using your account and its data.

Signing in before the deletion process has been initiated does not cancel your original request.

Once the deletion has been completed, your account and its associated data cannot be restored.

We respect your decision and appreciate the trust you've placed in JajanTracker.
Thank you for being part of our journey! 🤍
