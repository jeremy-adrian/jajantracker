1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • What is JajanTracker?
    JajanTracker is a personal finance and budgeting app that helps you monitor your spending and build better money habits.
  • How do I add a spending activity?
    Start by creating a budget in the Wallet menu. Once set, you can begin logging your daily expenses via the Activity menu. When adding a spending activity, choose the appropriate category from the available options and enter the spending details. This helps you organize and monitor your spending habits.
  • Is my data secure?
    Yes. We apply security measures and encryption practices to help protect your personal data. However, no online service can guarantee complete security, so please keep your account credentials safe.
  • What is the Message feature?
    The Message feature allows JajanTracker to send and receive communication within the app. Messages may be used for communication between users and for important service updates.
  • Can I delete a message that I have sent?
    No. Messages that have been sent cannot be modified or deleted by users. Messages are stored as part of JajanTracker's communication history to maintain a consistent experience for all participants.
  • What happens to my messages if I delete my account?
    When you delete your account, your personal account information may be removed. Some communication history may remain as part of the JajanTracker service. If your identity is no longer available, your previous messages may no longer display your personal information.
  • Who can see my profile information?
    Certain profile information, such as your name, profile photo (if provided), and role assigned by JajanTracker, may be visible to other users when needed for app features, such as viewing message sender information.
